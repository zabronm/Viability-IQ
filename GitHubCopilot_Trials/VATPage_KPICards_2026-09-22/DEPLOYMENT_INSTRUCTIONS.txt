DESIGN

- Smooth lift, shadow, icon and decoration transitions.
- Marble-blue border on hover.
- Six columns on wide screens, three on medium screens, two on tablets and one
  on narrow mobile screens.
- Reduced-motion support.

CALCULATIONS
- Output VAT: annual calculated output VAT.
- Input VAT: annual calculated input VAT.
- VATable Sales: annual taxable sales.
- Net VAT Payable: output VAT less input VAT.
- VATable Purchases: annual taxable purchases.
- VAT Recovery Rate: input VAT divided by output VAT.
