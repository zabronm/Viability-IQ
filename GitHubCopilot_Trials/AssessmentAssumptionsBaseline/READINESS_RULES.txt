ASSESSMENT PROJECTION READINESS RULES
=====================================

Purpose
-------
The readiness panel checks whether the minimum facts and assumptions needed for a
credible lightweight SME projection have been captured. It does not guarantee the
commercial success of a business and does not replace assessor judgement.

Required checks
---------------
1. Opening position
   Pass: At least one opening bank, asset, stock, debtor or creditor balance is present.
   Action: Confirm all opening balances, including valid zero balances.

2. Sales Categories
   Pass: At least one active Sales Category exists.
   Action: Add a category and assign the correct income type.

3. Monthly Sales
   Pass: At least one active Sales line contains a non-zero M1-M12 amount.
   Action: Enter the baseline monthly Sales/income forecast.

4. Debtor and creditor profile
   Pass: An active profile exists and its explicit ageing buckets do not exceed 100%.
   Action: Capture collection/payment timing. The unallocated balance is treated as current.

5. Operating expenses
   Pass: At least one active expense line exists.
   Action: Enter recurring and material operating expenses.

6. VAT status
   Pass: The assessment contains a VAT flag or a non-negative VAT rate.
   Action: Confirm VAT registration and the correct rate.

7. Core assumptions
   Pass: An active lightweight assumptions record exists.
   Action: Save the Sales, expense and funding assumptions.

Advisory checks
---------------
8. Loan details
   Pass: Every active loan has a usable balance, rate, term and M1-M12 start month.

9. Asset depreciation
   Pass: Every active depreciable asset has a positive rate and a method.

10. Baseline confirmation
    Pass: The assessor has marked the baseline assumptions as confirmed.

Status calculation
------------------
- Ready: no required failures and at least 90% of all checks pass.
- Ready with warnings: no required failures and fewer than 90% pass.
- Incomplete: one or more required checks fail.

The score is the number of passed checks divided by the ten checks. The projection
engine is intentionally unchanged in this package; readiness is an assessment-quality
control only.
