VIABILITYIQ ASSESSMENT BUDGETING - TWO-DECIMAL DISPLAY REFINEMENT
Package date: 2026-09-29

PREREQUISITE
------------
Deploy AssessmentBudgeting_2026-09-29 first.

CHANGE
------
All amounts rendered by the Assessment Budgeting page now use two decimal places:

- Editable monthly budget inputs
- Read-only monthly budget values
- Assessment projection values
- Monthly variance values
- Calculated rows
- Annual totals
- KPI amounts
- Signed comparison amounts

The database continues to retain DECIMAL(19,4) precision. This is a display-only
change and does not round or truncate persisted calculation values.

DEPLOYMENT
----------
Replace:

  ViabilityIQ.Web\Components\Pages_Assessments\AssessmentBudgetingPage.razor

No database script or service-registration change is required.

VALIDATION
----------
1. Open /assessment/budgets/{AssessmentId}.
2. Confirm editable values display as 1,620,000.00 rather than 1620000.0000.
3. Confirm calculated rows, Projection view, Variance view, totals and KPI cards
   all display two decimal places.
4. Edit and save a value; reload and confirm it remains displayed to two decimals.
