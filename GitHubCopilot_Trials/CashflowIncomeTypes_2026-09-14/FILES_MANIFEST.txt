CASHFLOW INCOME TYPE CLASSIFICATION PACKAGE
===========================================

Shared financial models
-----------------------
Files\ViabilityIQ.Shared\FinancialModels\IncomeSourceClassification.cs
  Maps active tblIncomeType names to stable cashflow income classifications.

Files\ViabilityIQ.Shared\FinancialModels\CashflowProjectionModels.cs
  Adds monthly and annual fields for each income source plus VAT-inclusive
  profit-summary values.

Cashflow engine
---------------
Files\ViabilityIQ.Database\FinancialCalculations\CashflowProjectionService.cs
  Loads active tblIncomeType rows and passes them to the calculator.

Files\ViabilityIQ.Database\FinancialCalculations\CashflowProjectionCalculator.cs
  Separates Sales, Asset Disposal, Grants/Donations, Sundry and Other income;
  corrects debtor, COGS, profit, cash inflow, ratio, and disposal behaviour.

Files\ViabilityIQ.Database\FinancialCalculations\CashflowEngine.cs
  Corrects compatibility DTO/entity totals to include all additional income.

Cashflow UI
-----------
Files\ViabilityIQ.Web\Components\Pages_Assessments\ProjectionComponents\CashflowSummaryDashboardComponent.razor
Files\ViabilityIQ.Web\Components\Pages_Assessments\ProjectionComponents\CashflowSummaryDashboardComponent.razor.css
  Implements the requested VAT-inclusive profit summary.

Files\ViabilityIQ.Web\Components\Pages_Assessments\ProjectionComponents\DetailedCashflowStatementComponent.razor
  Displays each classified income source separately in monthly cash inflows and
  VAT-exclusive profit lines.
