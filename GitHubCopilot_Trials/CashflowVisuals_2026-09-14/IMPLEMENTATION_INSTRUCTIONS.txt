CASHFLOW VISUALS - IMPLEMENTATION INSTRUCTIONS
==============================================

Purpose
-------
This package adds:

1. A live monthly performance graph showing:
   - Sales
   - Stock purchases
   - Operating expenses
   - Net cashflow
   - Net profit before tax

2. A business-performance indicator component showing:
   - Margin-of-safety assessment
   - Interest-cover assessment
   - Asset-efficiency assessment
   - Health score out of 100
   - Indicative business value
   - Monthly break-even
   - Margin of safety %
   - Interest cover times
   - Return on assets %

3. The requested responsive layout:
   - 12-Month Cash Summary: col-xl-4
   - Monthly Performance graph: col-xl-8

No production files were changed by this handoff package.


Prerequisite
------------
Apply these packages first:

1. CashflowIntegration_00
2. VATTransactions_FullImplementation_2026-09-14

The calculator in this package includes the live VAT integration.


Installation
------------
Copy every file beneath Files into the matching production path, replacing the
corresponding cashflow handoff files where present.

Then build:

    dotnet build ViabilityIQ.sln


Calculation definitions
-----------------------
Stock graph series:
  Net monthly AssessmentStock purchases.

Net profit graph series:
  Profit before tax = Revenue - COGS - operating expenses
                      - depreciation - interest.

Monthly break-even:
  Annual break-even sales / 12.

Margin of safety:
  (Annual revenue - annual break-even sales) / annual revenue.

Asset turnover:
  Annual revenue / average monthly projected total assets.

Return on assets:
  Annual profit before tax / average monthly projected total assets.

Projected total assets:
  Positive bank + debtors + stock + asset-movement net book values.

Indicative business value:
  NPV of the 12 monthly net cashflows using 10% nominal annual discounting.
  It is deliberately labelled indicative because this is not a full terminal
  value or market valuation.

Health score:
  Margin of safety       30 points
  Interest cover         25 points
  Current ratio          20 points
  Positive net cashflow  15 points
  Return on assets       10 points

The score is bounded from 0 to 100. A profitable business with no interest
expense receives the full interest-cover weighting because it has no interest
burden.


Behaviour
---------
All components receive the same immutable CashflowProjectionResult from
CashflowMainPagesComponent. Existing ProjectionStateManager invalidation
therefore refreshes the tables, graph, and indicators together whenever source
Sales, Stock, Expenses, VAT, Loan, Debtor/Creditor, or Asset data changes.

The chart is responsive SVG and introduces no JavaScript or NuGet dependency.
