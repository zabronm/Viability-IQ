ASSESSMENT DEBTORS / CREDITORS PROJECTION WORKSPACE
==================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This package adds a live-data, three-tab working-capital page:

1. Debtors
2. Creditors
3. Consolidated Outlook, followed by CashflowMainPagesComponent

No production file has been replaced. All implementation files are under:

    GitHubCopilot_Trials\AssessmentAccountsProjections\Files\


DATA BASIS CONFIRMED FOR THIS IMPLEMENTATION
--------------------------------------------
Opening debtors:
    Sum of AssessmentSalesCategory.OpeningDebtorsAmount for active categories.

Opening creditors:
    Sum of AssessmentSalesCategory.OpeningCreditorsAmount for active categories.

New debtors:
    Active AssessmentSales monthly values.

New creditors:
    Active AssessmentStock monthly values.

Collection/payment timing:
    Active DebtorsCreditorsProfile percentage fields:

    Debtors:
        Debtors_30
        Debtors_60
        Debtors_90
        Debtors_120
        Debtors_120Plus

    Creditors:
        Creditors_30
        Creditors_60
        Creditors_90
        Creditors_120
        Creditors_120Plus

    The DebtorsValue_* and CreditorsValue_* fields are not substituted for the
    requested percentage profiles.

VAT:
    When DebtorsCreditorsProfile.IncludeVAT is true, each sales or stock row's
    monthly values are multiplied by TotalWithVAT / TotalNoVAT. This preserves
    each row's actual annual VAT ratio instead of hard-coding a tax rate.

CALCULATION RULES
-----------------
Profile timing:

    0-30 days       delay 0 months
    31-60 days      delay 1 month
    61-90 days      delay 2 months
    91-120 days     delay 3 months
    120+ days       delay 4 months

Example:
If Month 1 sales are 100,000 and the debtor profile is 50% / 30% / 20%:

    Month 1 collections from those sales = 50,000
    Month 2 collections from those sales = 30,000
    Month 3 collections from those sales = 20,000

If your accounting policy treats "30 days" as collection in the following
month, change the delays in AccountsProjectionService.CreateBuckets from:

    0, 1, 2, 3, 4

to:

    1, 2, 3, 4, 5

Debtor formula per month:

    Closing debtors =
        Opening debtors
        + credit sales
        - collections
        - projected bad debt

Creditor formula per month:

    Closing creditors =
        Opening creditors
        + AssessmentStock purchases
        - supplier payments

Consolidated cash movement:

    Debtor collections - supplier payments

Amounts scheduled after Month 12 are not lost. They remain in the Month 12
closing debtor or creditor balance.

Bad debt:
The configured BadDebtPercentage applies to new projected sales. It does not
write off the brought-forward opening debtor balance. If opening debtors require
a separate impairment, add a dedicated opening-balance impairment input rather
than silently applying the new-sales assumption.

Invalid profile totals:
The saved percentages should total 100% independently for debtors and creditors.
If a non-zero total differs from 100%, the UI raises a warning and the service
normalizes those percentages to 100% for calculation. A zero total produces no
schedule and raises a critical alert.


ALERT RULES INCLUDED
--------------------
- No active debtor/creditor profile
- No active sales categories
- No monthly sales
- No monthly AssessmentStock purchases
- Debtor percentages do not total 100%
- Creditor percentages do not total 100%
- Bad debt at or above 5%
- Bad debt at or above 10%
- Closing creditors exceed closing debtors in at least 6 months
- Healthy/information state when no warning applies


IMPORTANT CASHFLOW INTERVENTION
-------------------------------
The Consolidated Outlook displays CashflowMainPagesComponent at the bottom as
requested.

However, the existing CashflowEngine currently uses monthly sales as immediate
SalesRevenue and does not consume debtor collections or creditor payments. This
means the existing cashflow component is visually consolidated on the new page,
but its internal cash receipts/payment calculations remain independent.

For accounting correctness, do not simply add collections on top of existing
SalesRevenue, because that would double count sales cash.

The correct later refactor is:

1. Inject IAccountsProjectionService into CashflowEngine.
2. Call CalculateAsync(assessmentId) once.
3. Use monthly DebtorCollections as operating cash receipts instead of treating
   all monthly credit sales as cash.
4. Use monthly CreditorPayments as stock-purchase cash outflows.
5. Ensure AssessmentStock purchases are not also included elsewhere in cash
   outflows.
6. Reconcile the new cashflow closing balance to the Consolidated Outlook.

This has deliberately not been forced into the package because cash/credit sales
classification is not currently present on AssessmentSales. Until that business
rule is defined, replacing all SalesRevenue with collections could incorrectly
defer cash sales.


UI CONTENT
----------
Parent page:
- Uses PageHeading_Assessment.
- Places Debtors, Creditors and Consolidated Outlook tabs immediately below the
  page heading.
- Uses the same tab markup, border, active underline, hover behavior, spacing,
  responsive behavior and print behavior as AssessmentAssetsPage.

All three tabs:
- Render their own AccountsProjectionAlerts instance immediately inside the tab.
- Use only M1, M2, M3 ... M12 in tables, chart axes and chart tooltips.

Debtors tab:
- Opening debtors, annual sales, collections, closing debtors and DSO KPIs
- Profile percentages and assumptions
- Collections versus closing-debtors chart
- 12-month movement matrix
- Sales-category distribution table

Creditors tab:
- Opening creditors, stock purchases, payments, closing creditors and payment
  coverage KPIs
- Profile percentages
- Payments versus closing-creditors chart
- 12-month movement matrix
- Purchase-category distribution table

Consolidated Outlook:
- Receipts, supplier payments, net cash movement, closing debtors and net
  working capital KPIs
- Receipts-versus-payments chart
- Monthly consolidated table
- Existing CashflowMainPagesComponent at the bottom

The charts use CSS only. No JavaScript file or Chart.js lifecycle management is
required.

6. Confirm annual purchases equal the active AssessmentStock monthly values.

8. Manually trace one stock value through its creditor timing buckets.

10. Confirm delayed Month 12 amounts remain in closing balances.
11. Confirm VAT-inclusive mode matches the source rows' annual VAT totals.
12. Refresh the browser and confirm the assessment ID remains in the URL.
