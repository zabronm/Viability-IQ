Change summary
--------------
- Places Standard and Open Plan choices in the first card.
- Places subscriber and conditional organisation fields in the second card.
- Places contact and security fields in the third card.
- Uses three equal-width columns on wide displays.
- Adapts to two plan choices plus stacked detail cards on tablet widths.
- Uses one column and a full-width submit button on mobile widths.
- Uses two equal columns when the page is opened in profile-edit mode.
- Preserves the existing registration events, validation, model bindings, and plan
  selection behavior.
- Removes the registration logo/header and compacts panel, field, footer, and card
  spacing so the form fits typical desktop screens.
- Keeps the MainLayout_Blank background fixed while its content surface scrolls.
- Restores automatic horizontal and vertical scrollbars when content exceeds the
  available viewport.
- Centers MainLayout_Blank content when it fits within the available viewport.
